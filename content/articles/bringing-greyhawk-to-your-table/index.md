> “Every miniature tells a story before the first die is rolled.”

For nearly fifty years, fantasy role-playing games have come alive through imagination. A Dungeon Master describes an ancient ruin, a wandering monster, or a mysterious stranger, and the players picture those scenes in their minds as the adventure unfolds.

Miniatures add another dimension to that experience.

Whether representing brave adventurers, fearsome dragons, loyal hirelings, or mysterious villains, a well-crafted miniature gives everyone around the table a shared vision of the world they are exploring. It becomes more than a game piece—it becomes a character with a story waiting to be told.

That simple idea inspired the creation of Oerik Foundry.

Oerik Foundry was created to produce high-quality 32 mm printable fantasy miniatures designed specifically for Adventures Dark & Deep™ and the World of Greyhawk. Rather than creating generic fantasy figures, every Oerik Foundry Mini is sculpted to represent the people, creatures, and locations that appear throughout the pages of OERIK Magazine. As the magazine grows, so too will the collection, allowing readers to build a tabletop world that reflects the adventures they read each month.

## Every Issue Includes a Free Oerik Foundry Mini

Beginning with Issue #1, every subscriber to OERIK Magazine will receive a free downloadable Oerik Foundry Mini.

These professionally sculpted STL files are designed for home resin printing or use with your favorite commercial 3D printing service. Simply download the file, print the miniature, paint it, and introduce it into your own Greyhawk campaign.

Each month's featured miniature will connect directly to the contents of the magazine. Sometimes it may be a heroic adventurer, sometimes a memorable non-player character, and other times a fearsome monster or loyal companion. Together they will create a growing collection of miniatures inspired by the people and places of Oerth.

## Featured Oerik Foundry Mini

### Lady Arcella

The inaugural Oerik Foundry Mini is Lady Arcella, the noblewoman of House Halvern.

Designed in heroic 32 mm scale, Lady Arcella is ready to serve as a trusted ally, influential patron, courageous adventurer, or beautifully painted display piece. Whether she appears in your own campaign or simply joins your miniature collection, she marks the beginning of what we hope will become a long tradition within the pages of OERIK.

**Included with Issue #1**

- STL miniature
- 32 mm heroic scale
- Compatible with Adventures Dark & Deep™ campaigns

## More Than Miniatures

Oerik Foundry is more than a collection of STL files.

In future issues, this department will explore the growing hobby of tabletop miniatures through articles on 3D printing, painting, terrain building, and bringing the World of Greyhawk to life. Whether you're printing your very first miniature or have years of experience behind the workbench, we hope these articles will help you create memorable gaming experiences while building a collection uniquely your own.

## Looking Ahead

Every issue of OERIK Magazine will introduce new Oerik Foundry Minis inspired by the adventures, personalities, monsters, and locations of Greyhawk.

Heroes.

Monsters.

As the world of OERIK expands, your tabletop collection can grow alongside it—one miniature at a time.
