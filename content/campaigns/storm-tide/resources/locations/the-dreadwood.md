The **Dreadwood** looms inland from the southern coast of Keoland, beyond the farms, marshes, and settled roads of Salinmoor.

It is a vast forest of ancient trees and deep wilderness. To those who know it well, the Dreadwood is not simply an empty stretch of woodland but a living region of hidden communities, old paths, wildlife, and places seldom seen by outsiders.

Two members of the Storm Tide adventuring company have especially close ties to the forest. **Haldir Sorenson** was raised within the Dreadwood and learned the ways of nature there as a druid and scout. **Galborn Valgwyn** grew up among an elven community in the forest and trained as a ranger and hunter.

Centaurs are also known to inhabit the forested hills along the Dreadwood's northwestern edge, and the party's travels have already begun to draw them toward the people and places found beneath its canopy.

Like many locations in the Storm Tide Campaign, the Dreadwood will become more detailed as the adventurers travel farther into it and discover what lies beyond the familiar paths.
