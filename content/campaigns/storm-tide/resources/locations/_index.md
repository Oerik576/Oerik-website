The **Storm Tide Campaign** reaches beyond any single town or adventure. This section records the places that have entered the campaign through travel, character history, local knowledge, rumor, or exploration.

Some entries describe places the adventurers know well. Others may begin with only a few facts and expand as the campaign reveals more.
