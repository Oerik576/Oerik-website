The **Storm Tide Campaign** has grown far beyond a single town or adventure. This section gathers the people, places, stories, discoveries, treasures, and other information that have become part of the campaign.

These pages are written from a **player-facing perspective**. Information appears here as it becomes known through play, and entries may grow as the adventurers learn more about the world around them.

Use these resources to revisit a place once explored, remember the name of someone met months ago, follow an old rumor, review a mysterious object, or recover information that may matter again later in the campaign.
