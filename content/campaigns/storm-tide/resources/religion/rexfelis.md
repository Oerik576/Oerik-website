## Overview

**Rexfelis**, sometimes referred to as the **Cat Lord**, is a quasi-deity of the World of Greyhawk with deep ties to felines, stealth, freedom, and independence. Though not a traditional member of the Oeridian pantheon, he is sometimes honored or recognized among them, particularly by rogues, wanderers, and feline-affiliated individuals or races.

## Description

Rexfelis is a powerful quasi-deity associated with cats, stealth, curiosity, and independence. He appears in various forms, most commonly as a charismatic humanoid feline, blending traits of panthers, lions, or domestic cats depending on the region and the viewer. He is aloof and enigmatic, much like the creatures he represents, and often interacts with the mortal world through emissaries.

In human form he always wears black clothing decorated with gold and gems of feline colors. He has a muscular, compact form, and his eyes are feline in whatever shape he takes.

## Relationships

Rexfelis maintains loose associations with powers of nature, trickery, and travel, such as **Olidammara**, **Ehlonna**, or **Fharlanghn**, but he is not considered a formal ally of any deity. He is known to oppose tyranny and domination, especially from gods like **Hextor** or **Vecna**, whose agendas threaten free-willed beings.

He is neither fully good nor evil, instead acting as a cosmic agent of balance through mischief and predatory grace.

## Realm

Rexfelis is said to dwell in the **Beastlands**, a plane of untamed wilderness and natural beauty. His personal demesne, often called the **Twilight Den**, is a shifting region of shadowed trees, ruined temples reclaimed by nature, and endless twilight where felines of all types roam freely.

It is rumored that every cat's soul, upon death, first walks the paths of his realm.

## Dogma

The Cat Lord is neither good nor evil, but is concerned solely with the welfare of cats. He travels between the Outer, Inner, and Material Planes in order to watch over his charges, leading them out of danger and sometimes assuming the leadership of feline prides—killing their previous leaders out of cold necessity.

Freedom is paramount. Rexfelis teaches that every creature must walk its own path, free of shackles—be they societal, divine, or arcane. Stealth, patience, and cunning are not vices but virtues to be honed. His faithful are encouraged to embrace curiosity, move unseen, and never let themselves be caged, physically or spiritually.

“Strike when ready, vanish when needed,” is a common tenet.

## Worshippers

Rexfelis is revered by:

- Rogues, bards, and shapeshifters
- Wizards with feline familiars
- Tabaxi, lycanthropes, or anyone bound to feline spirits
- Individuals in wild lands who venerate natural cunning and independence

## Clergy

Priests of Rexfelis are often called **Whispers** or **Shadowpads**. They rarely wear formal robes, instead donning loose, dark clothing suited to stealth, often adorned with feline motifs.

Most are wanderers, acting more like sacred spies or silent guardians than temple-bound clergy. They may serve as protectors of cats, informants in cities, or solitary monks living in the wild.

## Rituals

### The Night Purr

A vigil held under the new moon, in silence, as cats gather around.

### Nine Lives Offering

A small dish of cream or meat is left at crossroads or in abandoned ruins, often accompanied by whispered prayers.

### The Silent Leap

Initiates of the faith are blindfolded and must leap across a symbolic gap—or a real one—representing trust in instinct.

## Myths and Legends

### The Leap Between Shadows

A tale in which Rexfelis crossed between planes by leaping from one cat's eyes to another's, escaping pursuit by a divine hunter.

### The Broken Collar

A myth that Rexfelis was once a chained beast of another power, possibly Vecna or Nerull, but broke his bonds and swore no one would ever tame him or his kind again.

### Tikkit, the Two-Tailed Cat

A famous emissary of Rexfelis known to appear in times of danger, aiding those who treat cats with respect.
