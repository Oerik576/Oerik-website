For more than fifty years, fantasy role-playing games have invited players to gather around a table, imagine impossible worlds, and create stories that belong entirely to their own group of friends. Every campaign is different, but sooner or later every Game Master discovers a rules system that simply feels right—a game that supports the style of adventures they want to run.

For OERIK, that game is **Adventures Dark & Deep™**.

Built upon the foundations of First Edition fantasy role-playing, Adventures Dark & Deep feels immediately familiar to anyone who grew up exploring forgotten ruins, deciphering ancient maps, or wondering what waited beyond the next dungeon door. At the same time, it is not content to preserve the past unchanged. Instead, it continues the evolution of classic fantasy gaming, expanding its possibilities while remaining true to the principles that made it enduring in the first place.

The result is a game equally comfortable running a one-night dungeon expedition or a campaign that spans years of real time. Characters can rise from unknown adventurers to influential leaders, travel from isolated villages to the courts of kings, sail dangerous seas, explore other planes of existence, and leave their mark upon a living world that continues to evolve with every decision they make.

That vision makes Adventures Dark & Deep a natural companion for the **World of Greyhawk**. Both assume a setting larger than the player characters, where ancient civilizations have left behind mysteries waiting to be uncovered and where reputation is earned through hard choices rather than predetermined destiny. The rules exist to support those adventures, providing a framework that encourages exploration, imagination, and long-term campaign play.

Throughout the pages of OERIK, every adventure, monster, NPC, magic item, and campaign article will be written with Adventures Dark & Deep in mind. Our goal is not simply to publish game material, but to provide the tools and inspiration for years of memorable adventures around your own gaming table.

## First Edition, Continued

Adventures Dark & Deep™ is often described as "First Edition continued," and that simple phrase captures the heart of the game better than any list of features or mechanics ever could.

Rather than recreating a single moment in the history of fantasy role-playing, Adventures Dark & Deep begins with a different assumption: what if the original game had simply continued to grow and evolve? What new ideas might have been introduced? What systems would have been refined? What possibilities would have been explored while remaining true to the spirit that inspired generations of players?

The answer is a game that feels immediately familiar without ever feeling limited by nostalgia. The foundations remain recognizable—heroic adventurers, dangerous dungeons, powerful magic, and a world that rewards exploration and ingenuity—but those foundations have been carefully expanded with new options, additional depth, and a broader framework for long-term campaign play.

This philosophy sets Adventures Dark & Deep apart from many retro-clones. Its goal is not to preserve every rule exactly as it appeared decades ago, nor to replace the classic game with an entirely new design. Instead, it builds upon those traditions, respecting the assumptions that made First Edition enduring while embracing the idea that great games can continue to grow.

For longtime players, the transition feels remarkably natural. Familiar concepts remain intact, classic adventures require little or no modification, and the style of play that defined early fantasy role-playing remains at the heart of every campaign. At the same time, both players and Game Masters discover new possibilities that encourage fresh ideas without sacrificing the character of the original game.

In many ways, Adventures Dark & Deep succeeds because it looks in two directions at once. It honors more than fifty years of fantasy role-playing tradition while confidently continuing the journey, proving that a classic game can still surprise, inspire, and evolve without losing sight of what made it memorable in the first place.

## A Complete Fantasy Role-Playing System

One of the greatest strengths of Adventures Dark & Deep™ is its scope. Rather than dividing the game across numerous required supplements, the **Core Rulebook** and **Bestiary** together provide a complete toolkit for creating and running long-term fantasy campaigns.

For players, the Core Rulebook offers a remarkable range of possibilities. Familiar races and classes stand alongside expanded options, allowing every character to feel both classic and distinctive. Detailed equipment, combat, magic, secondary skills, and character backgrounds provide the structure needed to create heroes who naturally become part of the campaign world rather than simply visitors passing through it.

Game Masters will find just as much to explore. The rules extend far beyond dungeon expeditions, providing guidance for wilderness travel, settlements, hirelings, religions, encounters, ships and waterborne adventures, fortifications, and even the creation of entirely original campaign settings. Whether the next session takes place in a forgotten tomb, a bustling city, or aboard a ship crossing the Azure Sea, the tools are already there.

The companion Bestiary is equally ambitious. Drawing upon mythology, folklore, literature, and the traditions of classic fantasy gaming, it presents hundreds of creatures ranging from familiar monsters to unusual beings that can surprise even experienced players. Intelligent societies, humanoid spellcasters, optional rules, and thoughtful encounter design transform the Bestiary into far more than a collection of statistics—it becomes a sourcebook for adventure ideas and world building.

Taken together, these two volumes provide everything needed to support years of play. A campaign can begin with a handful of inexperienced adventurers exploring abandoned ruins and naturally grow into expeditions across continents, political struggles between rival kingdoms, voyages into the planes, and legendary quests that become part of the world's history. The rules never feel as though they are struggling to keep pace with the imagination of the Game Master; instead, they provide a solid foundation upon which every new adventure can be built.

## A Natural Home for Greyhawk

When the World of Greyhawk first appeared in 1980, it was presented as the official campaign setting for Advanced Dungeons & Dragons First Edition. The kingdoms, cultures, monsters, treasures, and legendary heroes that defined the Flanaess were created with those rules firmly in mind. The setting assumed a world of dangerous frontiers, mysterious ruins, ambitious rulers, secret societies, and adventurers who earned their reputations through courage, caution, and a little bit of luck.

Over the decades, Greyhawk continued to evolve. New products advanced the timeline beyond the Greyhawk Wars, introduced new political realities, and adapted the setting to later editions of the game. Each brought new ideas while remaining true to the world's rich history.

**OERIK returns to an earlier chapter.**

The articles, adventures, and campaign material found in these pages are rooted in **Common Year 576**, the era established by the original Folio and Boxed Set. It is a Greyhawk where the Temple of Elemental Evil still casts its shadow over the Kron Hills, Iuz threatens the north, the Scarlet Brotherhood remains a distant mystery, and countless unexplored ruins still wait for the first band of adventurers willing to light a torch and step inside.

Adventures Dark & Deep™ feels perfectly at home in that world because it shares the same assumptions that shaped Greyhawk from the beginning. Exploration is expected. Wilderness remains dangerous. Kingdoms pursue their own ambitions regardless of the player characters. Ancient magic is mysterious rather than commonplace, and even experienced adventurers must respect the unknown.

For Game Masters, this partnership offers a tremendous advantage. The classic adventures, sourcebooks, and articles published during the First Edition era can be used with minimal effort, preserving not only their mechanics but also the style of play for which they were originally written. The result is a campaign that feels authentic without being trapped in nostalgia—a living world where decades of published material can stand beside entirely new adventures created for OERIK.

That is why Adventures Dark & Deep serves as the official game of this magazine. It is more than a compatible rules system; it is the natural continuation of the style of fantasy role-playing that gave birth to Greyhawk itself.

## The Core Rulebook

![Adventures Dark & Deep Core Rulebook](/images/articles/why-adventures-dark-and-deep/adventures-dark-and-deep-core-rulebook.webp)

### For the Player

You begin by creating more than a collection of numbers—you create a place in the world.

The Core Rulebook presents a broad selection of races and more than twenty character classes, from familiar warriors, clerics, thieves, and magic-users to specialized professions such as the bard, mountebank, mystic, jester, and savant. Secondary skills, social class, languages, literacy, family background, and starting wealth all combine to produce characters with a history before the first adventure begins.

Equipment receives equal attention. Weapons, armor, adventuring gear, mounts, transportation, hirelings, and services provide the practical tools needed for life on the frontier, while an extensive magic system offers hundreds of spells spanning every level of play. Combat remains rooted in classic First Edition traditions while introducing expanded options that reward tactical thinking without overwhelming the table.

By the time a character leaves town for the first adventure, they feel like a believable inhabitant of the campaign world rather than a blank slate waiting for experience points.

### For the Game Master

The Core Rulebook is equally ambitious on the other side of the screen.

Dungeon exploration, wilderness travel, random encounters, weather, movement, visibility, and overland expeditions are all supported with practical rules designed to keep the focus on the adventure rather than constant improvisation. The book provides guidance for treasure placement, non-player characters, hirelings, followers, settlements, fortifications, and campaign economics while also exploring religion, deities, the outer planes, and the structure of a fantasy universe.

The Game Master is never limited to running a single style of adventure. One session may involve political intrigue in a great city, the next a desperate sea voyage through pirate-infested waters, and the following week an expedition into an ancient tomb hidden beneath forgotten ruins. The rules provide a consistent framework that supports each of those experiences without requiring additional sourcebooks.

Perhaps most valuable of all is the advice found throughout the volume. Rather than presenting rigid procedures, Adventures Dark & Deep encourages Game Masters to build living worlds, make fair rulings, and create campaigns that develop naturally through the actions of the players.

### For the Campaign

Many role-playing games excel at creating characters. Fewer provide the tools needed to sustain years of adventures.

Adventures Dark & Deep was written with the assumption that campaigns grow. Heroes travel beyond the local dungeon, kingdoms rise and fall, religions influence politics, ships cross dangerous seas, and rumors lead to places that no map has ever recorded. The Core Rulebook supports that progression from beginning to end, allowing a single campaign to evolve from a handful of first-level adventurers into a living world filled with allies, enemies, organizations, castles, and legends.

That breadth is what makes the book so compelling. It is not simply a player's handbook or a Game Master's guide. It is a complete fantasy role-playing system designed to support every stage of a classic campaign.

### For the Greyhawk Referee

From its very beginning, Greyhawk was built alongside Advanced Dungeons & Dragons First Edition. The assumptions of the setting and the assumptions of the rules evolved together, creating a style of fantasy role-playing where exploration, politics, religion, wilderness travel, and dangerous dungeons all existed as equally important parts of the campaign.

Adventures Dark & Deep™ embraces that same philosophy.

The extensive character options allow the Flanaess to feel like a living world populated by more than warriors and wizards alone. Bards entertain in the inns of Greyhawk City, mountebanks travel the roads as charming confidence artists, mystics seek enlightenment in distant monasteries, and savants devote their lives to the study of ancient civilizations. The result is a setting that feels broader and more believable without sacrificing the classic character of First Edition.

> "Every great campaign begins with a map, a handful of dice, and a rules system that encourages exploration and inspires imagination."

The campaign systems are equally well suited to Greyhawk. Secondary skills, social class, languages, and literacy help define where characters fit within society before the first adventure begins. A noble from Furyondy, a merchant's son from Dyvers, a hunter from the Gnarley Forest, and a sailor from Gradsul immediately feel different, not because of combat statistics, but because the rules encourage them to be products of the world they inhabit.

Religion receives similar attention. The rules naturally support campaigns where temples are centers of political and social influence, where priests serve distinct deities with unique traditions, and where faith shapes kingdoms as much as armies and kings. For Greyhawk referees using the rich pantheon of Oeridian, Suel, Flan, Baklunish, and Olman gods, these systems add depth to every encounter without unnecessary complexity.

Even travel feels at home in the Flanaess. Wilderness exploration, overland journeys, ships and waterborne adventures, encounters, weather, and the practical concerns of life on the frontier encourage the kind of campaigns that Greyhawk has always done best. A party might spend one month clearing forgotten ruins in the Cairn Hills, the next sailing the Azure Sea, and the next negotiating with nobles in Rel Astra—all using the same unified rules system.

Perhaps most importantly, Adventures Dark & Deep never forces Greyhawk to conform to the rules. Instead, the rules support the setting that already exists. They provide the framework while allowing the history, cultures, politics, mysteries, and personalities of the Flanaess to remain the true stars of the campaign.

For a referee choosing to run Greyhawk in **Common Year 576**, that partnership feels remarkably natural. The result is not simply a compatible rules system, but one that captures the atmosphere, assumptions, and style of play that defined the setting from its very beginning.

## The Bestiary

![Adventures Dark & Deep Core Bestiary](/images/articles/why-adventures-dark-and-deep/adventures-dark-and-deep-bestiary.webp)

### More Than a Monster Book

A great fantasy campaign is remembered for the places the heroes explore and the creatures they encounter along the way. The Adventures Dark & Deep™ Bestiary understands that monsters are far more than collections of Armor Class and Hit Dice—they are the inhabitants of a living world.

Nearly a thousand creatures fill its pages, ranging from the iconic monsters that defined classic fantasy gaming to forgotten beings drawn from mythology, folklore, and decades of role-playing history. Dragons, demons, devils, giants, undead, elementals, dragons, goblins, trolls, and countless other familiar foes stand beside strange new discoveries waiting to surprise even the most experienced players.

### For the Player

One of the quiet strengths of the Bestiary is that it restores a sense of discovery.

Veteran players often know exactly how to fight an owlbear, avoid a rust monster, or survive a gelatinous cube. Adventures Dark & Deep expands that world with hundreds of additional creatures, unusual variants, forgotten classics, and original creations that cannot be defeated by metagame knowledge alone.

Every expedition once again becomes an opportunity for genuine exploration. The strange silhouette moving through the trees may be a familiar monster—or something entirely new that demands caution, investigation, and ingenuity.

### For the Game Master

The Bestiary is organized as a campaign resource rather than a simple catalog of opponents.

Entire societies are represented alongside individual monsters. Humanoid tribes, intelligent races, celestial beings, fiends, elementals, dinosaurs, giant insects, plants, fungi, undead, and creatures from mythology provide enough variety to populate virtually any environment imaginable.

Need a forgotten temple guarded by archons? A primeval valley filled with dinosaurs? A haunted battlefield overrun by restless spirits? A subterranean kingdom of derro and grimlocks? A voyage across the Azure Sea threatened by sahuagin, sea dragons, and krakens? The Bestiary already provides the foundation.

### For the Greyhawk Referee

This is where the Bestiary truly shines.

Greyhawk has always been a world of ancient civilizations built upon the ruins of even older ones. The Flanaess is home to forgotten Suel cities buried beneath the Sea of Dust, mysterious forests where fey still hold court, isolated valleys where dinosaurs survive, and mountain ranges inhabited by giants, dragons, and things that civilized lands have long since forgotten.

The breadth of the Bestiary allows those regions to feel genuinely different from one another. A journey into the Dreadwood should not resemble an expedition into the Bright Desert or the Crystalmist Mountains, and this collection provides the creatures needed to give every region its own identity and atmosphere.

Just as importantly, the book preserves many classic monsters that defined the earliest days of fantasy role-playing while introducing enough unfamiliar creatures to ensure that even veteran players cannot assume they already know every danger waiting beyond the next door.

### A Living World

Perhaps the greatest compliment that can be paid to the Adventures Dark & Deep Bestiary is that it encourages referees to think beyond encounters.

It suggests ecosystems instead of wandering monster tables, civilizations instead of isolated stat blocks, and mysteries instead of predictable battles. The result is a world that feels inhabited rather than populated place where every ruined tower, forgotten temple, and unexplored wilderness hex promises the possibility of discovering something remarkable.

For a Greyhawk campaign, that sense of discovery may be the greatest treasure of all.

## A Living Game

One of the greatest advantages of Adventures Dark & Deep™ is that it is not simply a recreation of a classic rules system preserved in time. It is an active game that continues to evolve, receiving new adventures, sourcebooks, optional rules, monsters, and campaign material while remaining firmly rooted in the traditions of First Edition fantasy role-playing.

That philosophy mirrors the way many campaigns develop around the gaming table. No Game Master begins with every kingdom mapped or every mystery solved. New ideas appear over time, favorite non-player characters return, forgotten ruins become important years later, and a single adventure can grow into an entire campaign. Adventures Dark & Deep embraces that same spirit of gradual expansion, allowing the game to grow without abandoning the foundations that made it compelling in the first place.

For the Greyhawk referee, this provides tremendous flexibility. The Core Rulebook and Bestiary contain everything needed to run a complete campaign, but additional supplements allow Game Masters to explore subjects in greater depth whenever they choose. Whether the focus is monsters, magic, adventures, world building, or specialized rules, new material is designed to complement the existing system rather than replace it.

That approach is one of the reasons OERIK selected Adventures Dark & Deep as its official game. The magazine is not built around a finished collection of books that will never change, but around a living system that continues to inspire new ideas and new adventures.

Future issues of OERIK will occasionally shine a spotlight on these supplements, exploring the ways they can enrich a Greyhawk campaign while always keeping the Core Rulebook and Bestiary at the heart of the game. Our goal is not to encourage readers to collect endless rulebooks, but to help Game Masters discover the resources that best support the style of campaign they want to create.

In many ways, that is the greatest strength of Adventures Dark & Deep. It respects more than fifty years of fantasy role-playing tradition while continuing to build upon it, proving that a classic game can still surprise, inspire, and grow.

## The Adventure Continues

Whether you are returning to Greyhawk after decades away or discovering the Flanaess for the very first time, Adventures Dark & Deep™ provides a foundation upon which years of memorable campaigns can be built. It respects the traditions that shaped the earliest days of fantasy role-playing while embracing the idea that those traditions can continue to grow, evolve, and inspire new generations of players.

For OERIK, that philosophy is more than a rules system, it is a way of approaching the hobby. We believe that worlds should be explored rather than completed, mysteries should invite investigation rather than provide immediate answers, and the greatest stories are the ones created through the unexpected decisions of the people gathered around the gaming table.

Every issue of OERIK will build upon that foundation. Within these pages you will find adventures set in the World of Greyhawk, memorable non-player characters, dangerous monsters, forgotten ruins, magical treasures, campaign advice, historical articles, and new ideas designed to enrich long-term play. Some will expand upon the Core Rulebook and Bestiary, while others will explore the growing library of Adventures Dark & Deep supplements and the many ways, they can enhance a campaign.

Most importantly, we hope this magazine inspires you to play.

Unfold a map of the Flanaess. Gather a few friends around the table. Roll the dice. Explore the forgotten places beyond the next hill, the next forest, or the next dungeon door.

There are kingdoms yet to discover, legends yet to be written, and adventures waiting to become memories that will still be shared years from now.
