Arenir Stormrider was born in a small coastal village known for its seafaring traditions. From a young age, he felt a strong affinity with the ever-changing winds and the freedom of the open sea. Arenir grew up listening to tales of legendary heroes who fought against oppressive forces and championed individual freedoms.

When he was still young, Arenir was sent to live with a noble family in Gradsul. The family was rich by the standards of someone from a small village, but rather poor in relation to the nobility of the vast city.

As he reached adolescence, Arenir's adventurous spirit led him to question the rigid social structures that confined city life. He witnessed firsthand the struggles of the common people and the injustices perpetuated by those in power. These experiences fueled his desire to fight for a world where every individual could live free from oppression.

Arenir sought a deity who shared his passion for individual freedom and rebellion against tyranny. He found solace in the teachings of Kelanen, the hero-deity of swords and balance.

Armed with his trusty longsword and a heart filled with a burning desire for justice, Arenir set off on his journey as a champion. He sought to challenge the status quo, protect the weak, and stand against those who would impose their will upon others.
