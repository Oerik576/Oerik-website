The World of Greyhawk has never lived only in rulebooks and boxed sets.

For decades, referees, players, writers, researchers, and fans have kept the setting alive by discussing its history, exploring its mysteries, sharing campaign ideas, and introducing new generations of gamers to the Flanaess. Today, much of that conversation takes place through YouTube channels, livestreams, podcasts, and other community-created programs.

The Oerik Dispatch now has a place to collect them.

## Greyhawk Channels & Podcasts

A new **Greyhawk Channels & Podcasts** section has been added to the Greyhawk portion of the website. It is intended as a growing directory of shows and creators that maintain a continuing focus on the World of Greyhawk.

This is not meant to be a complete catalog of every video or podcast that has ever mentioned Greyhawk. Instead, the goal is to highlight useful, active, or historically significant resources that Greyhawk referees and players may want to explore.

The directory begins with six resources that approach Greyhawk and old-school fantasy gaming in very different ways.

### Greyhawk Grognard

**Joseph Bloch's Greyhawk Grognard** has long been one of the most recognizable voices devoted to Greyhawk and old-school fantasy gaming. The channel explores setting lore, classic sourcebooks and adventures, campaign ideas, and original material for Oerth.

[Visit Greyhawk Grognard on YouTube →](https://www.youtube.com/@GreyhawkGrognard)

### Lord Gosumba

**Lord Gosumba** offers a long-running mixture of Greyhawk actual play and discussion. The channel includes the *Order of Ulek* campaign, *Gabbin' at Lord Peak's Haven*, and the *Legends & Lore* series, bringing together campaign experience, setting discussion, and guests from throughout the Greyhawk community.

[Visit Lord Gosumba on YouTube →](https://www.youtube.com/@LordGosumba)

### 3orcs

**3orcs** provides Greyhawk campaign material with a strong focus on Verbobonc, the Village of Hommlet, the Temple of Elemental Evil, sandbox play, maps, and practical referee resources. It is especially useful for anyone developing adventures in one of Greyhawk's most familiar campaign regions.

[Visit 3orcs on YouTube →](https://www.youtube.com/@3orcs)

### Tenkar's Tavern

**Tenkar's Tavern**, hosted by Tenkar, is a long-running source of OSR and B/X D&D news, reviews, commentary, and investigation. A gamer since 1980 and a retired NYPD investigative sergeant, Tenkar brings a direct, independent perspective to old-school gaming, with regular coverage of classic rules, Old School Essentials, B/X play, and the wider tabletop industry.

Although the channel is not devoted exclusively to Greyhawk, its strong focus on classic fantasy role-playing and the OSR makes it a useful companion resource for Greyhawk referees and players interested in the broader old-school gaming community. The channel also features frequent videos and livestreams, along with an ongoing Open Table campaign for members.

[Visit Tenkar's Tavern on YouTube →](https://www.youtube.com/@TenkarsTavern)

### The DM and Friends

**The DM and Friends**, hosted by DM Angelo with a rotating cast of veteran adventurers, is devoted to old-school role-playing with a strong emphasis on **1st Edition AD&D, the World of Greyhawk, Gary Gygax, and classic Dragon Magazine**. The channel combines Greyhawk lore, Dungeon Master techniques, retrospectives, interviews, convention coverage, gaming news, and discussions of other classic role-playing games.

Its Greyhawk coverage makes it especially relevant to this directory. Alongside the video channel, **Hommlet Dark** provides a dedicated blog with additional Greyhawk-focused material, making The DM and Friends useful both for viewers looking for long-form discussion and referees who prefer written campaign and setting material.

[Visit The DM and Friends on YouTube →](https://www.youtube.com/@TheDMandFriends)

[Visit the Hommlet Dark Greyhawk blog →](https://blog.hommlet-dark.com/)

### Greycast

For those who prefer audio, **Greycast**, hosted by wilyhobbit and Matteus, offers an extensive archive of Greyhawk discussion. Episodes examine nations, organizations, personalities, classic adventures, and campaign ideas one subject at a time, making the show both entertaining listening and a useful research resource.

[Listen to Greycast →](https://wilyhobbit.podbean.com/)

## A Section That Will Continue to Grow

These six entries are only the beginning.

There are many people creating worthwhile Greyhawk material, and the directory will expand as additional channels, podcasts, livestreams, and shows are reviewed and added. The purpose is not simply to provide links, but to help readers discover voices that can deepen their understanding of the setting or inspire something useful at their own gaming table.

That fits the larger purpose of The Oerik Dispatch. Greyhawk is more than a collection of old publications. It remains a setting actively explored by people who continue to research it, play in it, argue about it, expand it, and share their enthusiasm with others.

The new directory gives those conversations a permanent home within the website.
