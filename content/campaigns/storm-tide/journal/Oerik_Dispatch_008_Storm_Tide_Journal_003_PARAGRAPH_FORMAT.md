# Campaign Journal 003 — The Menace of Blixteem

**Seaton's Cove, Duchy of Salinmoor — 10 Readying, 576 CY**<br>
**Session 3 — September 9, 2023**

**Adventurers:** **Arenir Stormrider**, **Cyr Baggins**, **Haldir Sorenson**, **Hugo Baudelaire**, **Galborn Valgwyn**, **Darrak Goldenforge**<br>
**Level:** 1st<br>
**Adventure:** RX-01B — *The Menace of Blixteem*

The first time the adventurers entered the mushroom cavern beneath Seaton's Cove, the young green dragon drove them back to the surface. This time they were returning with more knowledge, a stronger company, and six **Potions of Viridian Defense** created from the rare ingredients recovered by **Haldir Sorenson** and **Galborn Valgwyn**. They had spent weeks preparing for another confrontation, and now the dragon was still below waiting for them.

## One Last Meeting at the Salty Net

On the evening before the descent, the party gathered in a private room at the **Salty Net & Fishery Hall**, where **Tamily Tanderveil** kept food and drinks moving around the table while the adventurers reviewed their plans. The meeting also brought the expanded company together in one place for the first time. **Haldir Sorenson** introduced **Galborn Valgwyn**, the elven ranger who had accompanied him through the Fogfen and into the Good Hills in search of the ingredients needed for the Viridian Defense potions, while **Arenir Stormrider** introduced **Darrak Goldenforge**, a dwarven fighter whose abilities had been vouched for by **Captain Lardus Longsaddle**.

Over the course of the evening, **Haldir** and **Galborn** recounted their journey and the dangers they had faced to secure the potion ingredients. **Arenir** shared what he had learned while training at the garrison, particularly the importance of fighting as a coordinated group rather than as six individuals acting independently. The mood remained serious, but the gathering was not grim; the party knew what waited below, and for the first time they also knew something about how to survive it.

## Council at Menhemes Manor

The following morning, the party made one final visit to **Mayor Oseph Menhemes** at the Menhemes estate. **Captain Lardus Longsaddle** reported that the entrance beneath the Salty Net had remained secured, while **Worliwynn** confirmed that the Potions of Viridian Defense were ready. Then **Lyrielle Silivren** offered a warning: she had seen troubling signs and believed the green dragon might be only one piece of a larger danger gathering around Seaton's Cove.

**Mayor Oseph Menhemes** had another concern. The **Founders' Fair** was only a few weeks away, and in Seaton's Cove the fair traditionally marked the beginning of the mayoral election season, when prospective candidates announced their intentions and began seeking support among the townsfolk. Oseph had heard rumors that the blacksmith **Carman Rajani** intended to announce a challenge for the mayor's office, and a disaster during the fair would not only endanger the town but could immediately become an issue in the coming political campaign.

For the moment, however, there was only one problem the adventurers could solve directly: the dragon beneath the town.

## Tharion's Request

As the group prepared to leave, **Worliwynn** passed along a message from the cleric-alchemist **Tharion**. If the party succeeded in killing the dragon, he wanted the body brought back so that he could study it and determine whether additional elixirs might be created from its remains. **Tharion** could offer only **50 gold pieces** for the corpse, but if his experiments succeeded, he promised each member of the company one of the resulting potions.

The request was unusual, but practical. If they survived the battle, they would bring the dragon back.

## Back Into the Caverns

Later that morning, the party returned to the **Salty Net & Fishery Hall** and descended through the basement into the underground complex. A heavy door on the lower level had been spiked shut after their previous expedition, and removing the spikes quietly proved difficult. Once the way was open, **Arenir Stormrider** entered first.

The chamber beyond was familiar, but something was wrong. The kobold bodies the party had left behind were gone. **Hugo Baudelaire** noticed the smell of death lingering in the room, and **Haldir Sorenson** quickly realized that every corpse had disappeared. A trail of blood led deeper into the complex toward the dragon's lair, and in the next chamber **Cyr Baggins** detected the same deathly odor, though it was weaker there.

Something had moved through the caverns since the party's last visit, and whatever had taken the dead had carried them toward the dragon.

## The Dragon's Lair

The trail led back to the vast natural cavern where the previous expedition had ended. Towering yellow mushrooms cast an eerie glow across the stone floor, broken barrels, scattered debris, and kobold bones. **Arenir Stormrider** and **Darrak Goldenforge** moved to the front while the others spread out behind them, with **Galborn Valgwyn** and **Cyr Baggins** searching for firing positions and **Hugo Baudelaire** and **Haldir Sorenson** preparing to support the fighters.

Then **Blixteem** attacked.

The warriors met the dragon at close range. Many blows glanced away from its hide, but enough struck home to prove the creature could be hurt. In return, **Blixteem's** claws and teeth tore into the front line whenever an opening appeared. Behind them, **Hugo** unleashed magic missiles while **Galborn** and **Cyr** maneuvered for clear shots, and when his spells offered little immediate help, **Haldir** closed with his spear.

The battle quickly became as much about position as strength. **Arenir** and **Darrak** tried to keep the dragon occupied while the others searched for angles from which they could attack without striking their companions. Then **Blixteem** changed the fight by leaping onto the top of one of the giant mushrooms and releasing a cloud of poisonous gas across the cavern.

## Viridian Defense

The breath weapon should have turned the battle, but instead the adventurers remained standing. The **Potions of Viridian Defense** had worked.

The dragon had gained height, but it had also exposed itself. **Arenir** and **Darrak** could no longer reach it with their weapons, while **Hugo**, **Galborn**, **Cyr**, and **Haldir** could attack from a distance. Arrows and spells began finding their mark, including one of **Galborn's** shafts that penetrated the dragon's hide.

Believing its poisonous breath had crippled the warriors below, **Blixteem** returned to the melee and discovered its mistake too late. **Arenir** and **Darrak** were still on their feet, and the party closed around the dragon again.

## Blixteem Falls

The dragon fought savagely as the adventurers pressed their advantage. **Arenir Stormrider** and **Darrak Goldenforge** kept it occupied at close range, while **Galborn Valgwyn** and **Cyr Baggins** attacked whenever openings appeared. **Hugo Baudelaire** continued using magic, and **Haldir Sorenson** remained close enough to strike with his spear.

Slowly, the wounds accumulated until **Haldir** found his opening and drove his spear home, delivering the final blow. **Blixteem** collapsed among the glowing mushrooms, and the creature that had once sent the party fleeing through the tunnels was finally dead.

## Beyond the Lair

After tending to their wounds, the adventurers searched the cavern and discovered the ledge where **Blixteem** had made its resting place. They also found another passage leading away from the lair, but the route ended at a recent collapse. Broken stone and rubble blocked the passage, almost certainly another result of the earthquake that had opened the underground complex in the first place.

Rather than risk bringing more of the caverns down around them, the party turned back. Whatever lay beyond the collapse would have to wait.

## Return to the Surface

There was still **Tharion's** request to fulfill. Dragging a dead dragon through an underground dungeon proved considerably less glorious than killing one, but the adventurers eventually hauled **Blixteem's** body back through the passages and up into the basement of the Salty Net.

They had returned below with knowledge, preparation, and a plan, and this time it had been enough. The young green dragon was dead, the Viridian Defense potions had proven their worth, and the immediate threat beneath Seaton's Cove had been ended. Yet **Lyrielle Silivren's** warning remained: the dragon might have been only one piece of something much larger.
