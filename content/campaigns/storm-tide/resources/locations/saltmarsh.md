**Saltmarsh** is a coastal town tied closely to the sea and maritime trade. It is known in the Storm Tide Campaign in part through **Cyr Baggins**, who left his childhood home of Seaton's Cove and spent part of his youth there.

Cyr remembers Saltmarsh as a tight-knit community where seemingly everyone knew one another. Its position along the coast brought sailors, merchants, and stories of smugglers and rogues, and it was there that Cyr learned many of the skills he later carried home to Seaton's Cove.

The campaign has not yet revealed everything Saltmarsh may hold. This entry will grow as the town becomes more important to the adventurers.
