## Overview

Worliwynn is the wise forest gnome druid of **Stone Ring Pond**, Haldir's storm-magic mentor, a respected spiritual guide of [Seaton's Cove]({{< ref "/campaigns/storm-tide/resources/locations/seatons-cove.md" >}}), and one of the foremost guardians of the natural balance along the southern coast of **Salinmoor**.

She serves as a bridge between civilization and the wild places of the region, particularly [the Dreadwood]({{< ref "/campaigns/storm-tide/resources/locations/the-dreadwood.md" >}}), the marshlands, and the natural forces surrounding Seaton's Cove.

## Physical Description

Worliwynn is a small forest gnome whose appearance reflects her deep connection to the natural world.

She has wild white hair woven with twigs, beads, and tiny seashells. Her bright hazel eyes seem constantly alert to signs and omens that others might overlook. She wears a cloak of mossy green and earthen brown, along with clothing fashioned from woven reeds, natural fibers, and enchanted leaves.

She looks far more at home in the forest, marsh, and wilderness than within the streets of town.

### Haldir Sorenson

Worliwynn played a critical role in Haldir's development as a druid.

After Haldir's early training in [the Dreadwood]({{< ref "/campaigns/storm-tide/resources/locations/the-dreadwood.md" >}}), his mentor **Eolara Earthheart** directed him to seek out Worliwynn because of her expertise with storm magic. Worliwynn became one of Haldir's principal teachers and helped him develop his storm-born druidic abilities.

She remains one of the most influential figures in his life.

## Role in the Campaign

Worliwynn occupies a unique position among the influential figures of Seaton's Cove.

While others lead through political authority, military command, trade, or scholarship, Worliwynn's influence comes from wisdom, experience, and her connection to the natural world.

If Vandy Banderdash represents knowledge and learning, Worliwynn represents nature, intuition, and ancient traditions.

She has repeatedly shown a willingness to act when either the community or the natural balance is threatened.

## Stone Ring Pond

Worliwynn resides near **Stone Ring Pond**, north of Seaton's Cove.

The pond serves as:

- A druidic sanctuary
- A place of natural magic
- A site for communion with nature
- A refuge from town life

The grove surrounding the pond is considered one of the notable natural landmarks of the region.

## Connection to Ley Lines

As a druid deeply attuned to the land, Worliwynn is highly sensitive to disturbances in the natural magical currents that flow through Salinmoor.

Although she may not understand such phenomena through arcane theory, she often senses when something in the land is out of balance.

Her awareness makes her one of the first people likely to recognize unusual magical disturbances affecting the region.
