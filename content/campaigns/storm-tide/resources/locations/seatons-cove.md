**Seaton's Cove** is a small coastal community in the Duchy of Salinmoor, tucked between the southern reaches of Keoland and the Azure Sea.

Fishing boats crowd its wharves. Merchants and sailors pass through its streets, while lumbermen, craftsmen, fishmongers, shipwrights, farmers, and dockworkers make up much of its population. Life follows the rhythm of tide, trade, and season, and the harbor provides the town's strongest connection to the wider world.

The settlement is civilized enough to provide lodging, supplies, craftsmen, temples, merchants, and ships, but that security does not extend far beyond town. Roads become trails, farms give way to wilderness, and the surrounding coast is marked by marshes, isolated coves, cliffs, ruins, and old stories.

Seaton's Cove has accumulated its share of local tales: shipwrecks, strange lights along the coast, forgotten structures, creatures in the marshes, and tunnels said to have been sealed generations ago. Some residents believe such stories. Others laugh at them. A few know better than to go looking.

It is here that the first adventurers of the **Storm Tide Campaign** came together.
