The **World of Greyhawk** is the primary campaign setting explored throughout The Oerik Dispatch. Our coverage begins in **Common Year 576**, using the classic World of Greyhawk setting as its foundation and expanding outward through history, cultures, regions, campaign material, player resources, and original OERIK content.

This section will grow into a practical Greyhawk reference for players and referees while preserving the open-ended character of the setting that makes the Flanaess such a rewarding place to explore.
