**Seaton's Cove, Duchy of Salinmoor --- Fireseek, 576 CY**\
**Campaign Journal 000 --- Campaign Prologue**

Before there was a dragon beneath the town, before strange things began
stirring along the coast, there were five adventurers in a small fishing
community on the southern coast of the Flanaess.

They were not yet heroes.

They had no famous victories behind them, no legendary treasures to
their names, and no reason to believe that the fate of Seaton's Cove
would soon rest partly in their hands.

This is where the **Storm Tide Campaign** begins.

<hr style="border:0; border-top:3px solid #000000; width:100%;">
## The Southern Coast of Keoland

The Duchy of Salinmoor lies along the southern coast of the Kingdom of
Keoland, where settled farmland and fishing villages give way to marsh,
forest, rocky headlands, and the broad waters of the Azure Sea.

It is a region shaped by the sea. Fishing boats leave sheltered coves
before sunrise. Coastal traders move goods between settlements. Beyond
the settled roads, however, the coast becomes increasingly wild.

The great Dreadwood looms inland. Marshes and fog-shrouded wetlands
stretch across the lowlands. Ruined structures stand along forgotten
stretches of coast, and sea caves honeycomb cliffs battered by storms
for centuries.

## Seaton's Cove

![Seaton's Cove, a small coastal community in the Duchy of Salinmoor.](images/campaigns/storm-tide/journals/Seatons-Cove-Header.png)

*Seaton's Cove on the southern coast of Keoland.*

Seaton's Cove is a small coastal community in the Duchy of Salinmoor,
tucked between the southern reaches of Keoland and the Azure Sea.

Fishing boats crowd its wharves. Merchants and sailors pass through its
streets. Lumbermen, craftsmen, fishmongers, shipwrights, farmers, and
dockworkers make up much of its population.

It is not a great city or a famous stronghold. That is part of its
appeal.

Life here follows the rhythm of tide, trade, and season. The harbor is
the town's connection to the wider world, while the nearby wilderness
provides much of its prosperity---and much of its danger.

## A Town with a Long Memory

Seaton's Cove may appear ordinary to a traveler passing through, but it
has existed long enough to accumulate its share of stories.

Some concern shipwrecks. Others tell of strange lights seen along the
coast, forgotten structures hidden in the wilderness, creatures
encountered in nearby marshes, or tunnels whose entrances were sealed
generations ago.

Most residents treat such stories exactly as townsfolk everywhere treat
old legends. Some believe them. Some laugh at them. And some simply know
better than to go looking.

## Life on the Edge

Seaton's Cove is civilized enough that travelers can find lodging,
supplies, craftsmen, temples, merchants, and ships. But civilization
does not extend very far beyond it.

The roads eventually become trails. The farms eventually become
wilderness. The familiar coastline gives way to isolated coves, cliffs,
marshes, and ruined places that appear on few maps.

For adventurers, this makes Seaton's Cove an ideal place to begin.

In Fireseek of 576 CY, there were five.

# The Adventurers

They did not arrive as a famous adventuring company. Each came to
Seaton's Cove by a different road and for different reasons.

Together, they would become the first company of the Storm Tide
Campaign.

## Arenir Stormrider

![Arenir Stormrider](images/campaigns/storm-tide/journals/Arenir.png)

**Human Champion --- Noble**

Arenir Stormrider came from a background that carried expectations. He
had been raised with an understanding of responsibility, martial
discipline, and the obligations that accompanied status.

Among the five adventurers, Arenir represented the most obvious martial
presence. Where others relied upon stealth, wilderness knowledge, or
magic, Arenir was prepared to meet danger directly.

## Galborn Valgwyn

![Galborn Valgwyn](images/campaigns/storm-tide/journals/Galborn.png)

**Elf Ranger --- Hunter**

Galborn Valgwyn was most at home where roads became trails. An elven
ranger and hunter, Galborn brought an understanding of wilderness
travel, tracking, woodcraft, and the creatures that inhabited the lands
beyond the town.

His bow gave the company reach. His experience gave them warning.

## Cyr Baggins

![Cyr Baggins](images/campaigns/storm-tide/journals/Cyr.png)

**Halfling Rogue --- Scout**

Small, quick, curious, and observant, Cyr Baggins possessed precisely
the sort of talents that caused sensible people to wonder how he had
gotten somewhere they had carefully locked.

Cyr was a scout and rogue whose strengths lay in stealth, investigation,
and getting into---or out of---places where others might struggle.

## Haldir Sorenson

![Haldir Sorenson](images/campaigns/storm-tide/journals/Haldir.png)

**Half-Elf Druid --- Scout**

Haldir Sorenson stood between two worlds. A half-elf druid with the
skills of a scout, he combined an understanding of wilderness survival
with a deeper connection to the natural world.

In a region where forest, swamp, coastline, and civilization constantly
pressed against one another, Haldir's perspective would prove especially
valuable.

## Hugo Baudelaire

![Hugo Baudelaire](images/campaigns/storm-tide/journals/Hugo.png)

**Human Wizard --- Barrister**

Hugo Baudelaire was perhaps the least conventional member of the
company. A wizard by training and a barrister by profession, Hugo
combined arcane study with an education rooted in argument, evidence,
interpretation, and law.

Magic gave him capabilities that none of his companions possessed. His
education gave him another weapon entirely: the ability to recognize
that many mysteries could be solved before anyone drew a sword.

# The Beginning

They were five 1st-level adventurers at the beginning of their careers.

No songs had been written about them. No tavern keeper offered them free
drinks because of deeds performed in distant lands. They had no
fortress, no great patron, and no pile of recovered treasure waiting to
finance the next expedition.

For the moment, Seaton's Cove was quiet.

That was about to change.

## The Night the Ground Moved

Late in the year 575 CY, a small earthquake shook Seaton's Cove.

Most buildings survived. Most residents returned to their normal
routines. But the **Thirsty Alpaca** collapsed.

Then the stories began.

Odd sounds. Unpleasant smells. Strange lights seen among the rubble.

Life in Seaton's Cove continued---for almost a month.

Then something began getting into the stores beneath the **Salty Net &
Fishery Hall**.

And someone decided it was time to find a few adventurers.
