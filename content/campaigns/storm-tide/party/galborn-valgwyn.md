Galborn Valgwyn was born and raised in a small elven community in the heart of the Dreadwood forest, among ancient trees and abundant wildlife. As an elf, he developed a natural affinity for the forest, its creatures, and its subtle magic.

From a young age, Galborn demonstrated a talent for tracking and hunting. Those skills eventually led him to become a ranger and a valuable protector of the Dreadwood.

Galborn's training was not limited to his own people. He had the opportunity to learn alongside the centaurs who inhabited the forested hills along the northwestern edge of the Dreadwood. They taught him the ways of the forest and shared their intimate knowledge of the land.

Through their guidance, Galborn refined his skills as a hunter and developed a deeper understanding of the balance between the creatures of the forest and the people who depend upon its resources, including the lumber industry.

Galborn now carries those lessons with him as he ventures beyond his home. Armed with his longbow and guided by the wisdom of the Dreadwood and its centaurs, he seeks to defend the forest against outside threats and preserve its beauty for future generations.
