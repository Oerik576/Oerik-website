Greyhawk Reference Library

The World of Greyhawk has been developed across decades of published material. These references provide the foundation for the version of Greyhawk used throughout The Oerik Dispatch, beginning in Common Year 576.

TSR 9025 — The World of Greyhawk Fantasy World Setting

Gary Gygax · 1980

The original published guide to the Flanaess and a principal reference for The Oerik Dispatch. The Folio establishes the nations, geography, rulers, populations, and political landscape of Greyhawk in Common Year 576.
